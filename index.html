The Manuscript

Twenty-five lives, one message

From Adam, the first man, to Muhammad, the final messenger — the Quran tells their stories as guidance for us. QASAS gathers them in one illuminated manuscript, each chapter anchored in the verses themselves.

Quran-first

Every chapter is built on real verses — shown in Arabic with Saheeh International translation and exact citations. Nothing invented, nothing embellished.

Reverent by design

No faces of the prophets are ever shown. Each story is told through symbols and landscapes — a staff, an ark, a well — in the spirit of the tradition.

Free, always

QASAS is a non-profit educational project. No paywalls, no ads, no monetised religious content — knowledge of the prophets belongs to everyone.

About

Why QASAS exists

The Quran calls the stories of the messengers a reminder and a lesson for people of understanding. QASAS is an attempt to gather those stories in one place — told plainly, anchored in the verses themselves, and designed with the reverence they deserve.

Every chapter shows its sources: the Arabic text of each verse, the Saheeh International translation, and the exact surah and verse number. Where the Quran is silent, we stay silent — no invented dialogue, no embellished details.

QASAS is a free educational project. It carries no ads and sells nothing; knowledge of the prophets should reach everyone.
