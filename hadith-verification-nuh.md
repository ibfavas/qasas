# Nuh (AS) — Hadith Verification Report
Verified 2026-10-01 against actual Sunnah.com page text (English translations read verbatim).
Scope: Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim only.

## SAHIH AL-BUKHARI — 7 hadiths (all VERIFIED on Sunnah.com)

### 1. Sahih al-Bukhari 4712 — Intercession (longest version)
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4712
- Book: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer); Narrator: Abu Huraira
- Key excerpt (Dr. Muhsin Khan translation), the Nuh portion:
  "So they will go to Noah and say (to him), 'O Noah! You are the first (of Allah's Messengers) to the people of the earth, and Allah has named you a thankful slave; please intercede for us with your Lord. Don't you see in what state we are?' He will say... 'I had (in the world) the right to make one definitely accepted invocation, and I made it against my nation. Myself! Myself! Myself! Go to someone else; go to Abraham.'"
- Usable facts: Nuh is "the first of Allah's Messengers to the people of the earth"; Allah named him "a thankful slave"; Nuh declines intercession because he already used his one accepted invocation — against his own nation.

### 2. Sahih al-Bukhari 3340 — Intercession (Prophets book version)
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3340
- Book: Prophets; Narrator: Abu Huraira
- **CORRECTION:** The task guessed this might be the "Did you convey?" hadith — it is NOT. It is an intercession variant.
- Key excerpt: "They will go to Noah and say; 'O Noah! You are the first amongst the messengers of Allah to the people of the earth, and Allah named you a thankful slave... Will you not intercede for us with your Lord?' Noah will reply: 'Today my Lord has become so angry as he had never been before and will never be in the future. Myself! Myself! Go to the Prophet (Muhammad).'"

### 3. Sahih al-Bukhari 4487 — "Did you convey?" (Tafseer of 2:143)
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4487
- Book: Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer), on verse 2:143; Narrator: Abu Sa'id Al-Khudri
- **This IS the "Did you convey the Message?" hadith.**
- Key excerpt: "Noah will be called on the Day of Resurrection and he will say, 'Labbaik and Sa'daik, O my Lord!' Allah will say, 'Did you convey the Message?' Noah will say, 'Yes.' His nation will then be asked, 'Did he convey the Message to you?' They will say, 'No Warner came to us.' Then Allah will say (to Noah), 'Who will bear witness in your favor?' He will say, 'Muhammad and his followers. So they (i.e. Muslims) will testify that he conveyed the Message.'"

### 4. Sahih al-Bukhari 3339 — "Did you convey?" (Prophets book, shorter)
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3339
- Book: Prophets; Narrator: Abu Sa'id
- Key excerpt: "Noah and his nation will come (on the Day of Resurrection) and Allah will ask (Noah), 'Did you convey (the Message)?' He will reply, 'Yes, O my Lord!' Then Allah will ask Noah's nation, 'Did Noah convey My Message to you?' They will reply, 'No, no prophet came to us.' Then Allah will ask Noah, 'Who will stand a witness for you?' He will reply, 'Muhammad and his followers (will stand witness for me).'"

### 5. Sahih al-Bukhari 7440 — Intercession (Anas version)
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:7440
- Book: Oneness, Uniqueness of Allah (Tawhid); Narrator: Anas, via Qatada
- Key excerpt: "He will add, 'Go to Noah, the first prophet sent by Allah to the people of the Earth.' The people will go to Noah who will say, 'I am not fit for this undertaking.' He will mention his mistake which he had done, i.e., his asking his Lord without knowledge."
- NOTE — genuine matn difference: in THIS narration Nuh's stated reason is "his asking his Lord without knowledge" (i.e., pleading for his son — cf. Qur'an 11:46–47), whereas the Abu Huraira versions (4712, 3340, Muslim 194a) cite his invocation/curse against his people. Both wordings are authentic; the chapter can note the narrations differ on which of Nuh's actions is mentioned here.

### 6. Sahih al-Bukhari 3337 — Nuh warned his nation against the Dajjal
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3337
- Book: Prophets; Narrator: Ibn Umar
- Key excerpt: "l warn you against him (i.e. the Dajjal) and there was no prophet but warned his nation against him. No doubt, Noah warned his nation against him but I tell you about him something of which no prophet told his nation before me. You should know that he is one-eyed, and Allah is not one-eyed."

### 7. Sahih al-Bukhari 3338 — "as Noah warned his nation"
- Link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3338
- Book: Prophets; Narrator: Abu Huraira
- Key excerpt: "The Dajjall is one-eyed and will bring with him what will resemble Hell and Paradise, and what he will call Paradise will be actually Hell; so I warn you (against him) as Noah warned his nation against him."

## SAHIH MUSLIM — 2 hadiths (both VERIFIED on Sunnah.com)

### 8. Sahih Muslim 194a (in-book: Book 1, Hadith 386) — Intercession
- Link: https://sunnah.com/muslim:194 (page text verified via the Book of Faith index; direct fetch returned a transient error, so the full text was read from the index page which reproduces it verbatim)
- Book: The Book of Faith (Kitab al-Iman); Narrator: Abu Huraira
- Key excerpt (Abdul Hamid Siddiqui translation): "They would come to Noah and would say: O Noah, thou art the first of the Messengers (sent) on the earth (after Adam), and Allah named thee as a 'Grateful Servant,' intercede for us with thy Lord. Don't you see in what (trouble) we are? Don't you see what (misfortune) has overtaken us? He would say: Verily, my Lord is angry today as He had never been angry before, and would never be angry afterwards. There had emanated a curse from me with which I cursed my people. I am concerned with only myself, I am concerned only with myself; you better go to Ibrahim (peace be upon him)."
- Sahih Muslim 194b is a shorter variant narration (different opening: meat/bread/soup setting); same Nuh content.

### 9. Sahih Muslim 169d (also cross-referenced 2930a, 2931) — Nuh warned against the Dajjal
- Link: https://sunnah.com/muslim:169d
- Book: The Book of Tribulations and Portents of the Last Hour; Narrator: Abdullah b. Umar
- Key excerpt: "I warn you of him and there is no Prophet who has not warned his people against the Dajjal. Even Noah warned (against him) but I am going to tell you a thing which no Prophet told his people. You must know that he (the Dajjal) is one-eyed and Allah, the Exalted and Glorious, is not one-eyed."

## CHECKED AND REJECTED (not about Nuh / out of scope)
- Bukhari 7510 (https://sunnah.com/bukhari:7510): intercession hadith (Anas, via Ma'bad bin Hilal), but this narration's chain goes Adam → Abraham → Moses → Jesus → Muhammad — **Nuh is not mentioned**. Rejected.
- Bukhari 4604 (https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4604): matn is about Yunus ("Whoever says that I am better than Jonah bin Matta, is a liar"); Nuh appears only in the chapter's Qur'anic verse heading (4:163). Rejected as not a Nuh hadith.
- Riyad as-Salihin 1866 and Musnad Ahmad 15: about Nuh / intercession but not Bukhari or Muslim — excluded per scope.
- Ark / flood: a web search for Bukhari/Muslim hadiths on the ark or the flood itself surfaced none.
