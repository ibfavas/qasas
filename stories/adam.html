Chapter I

Adam (AS) — The First Man

A khalifa on earth

Before mankind existed, Allah announced to the angels His decree: He would place upon the earth a successive authority — a khalifa. The angels, who knew only worship, asked whether He would place there one who would cause corruption and shed blood, while they exalted Him with praise. And Allah answered them with words that settle every question of wisdom beyond our sight: "Indeed, I know that which you do not know."

So Adam was created — the first man, the father of mankind — and honoured above much of creation. Allah taught him the names of all things and commanded the angels to prostrate to him. All obeyed except Iblis, who refused out of pride, and was cast out.

Verse

وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّى جَاعِلٌ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ خَلِيفَةً ۖ قَالُوٓا۟ أَتَجْعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفْسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسْفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحْنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمْدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَ ۖ قَالَ إِنِّىٓ أَعْلَمُ مَا لَا تَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٣٠﴾

And [mention, O Muhammad], when your Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority." They said, "Will You place upon it one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood, while we exalt You with praise and declare Your perfection?" He [Allah] said, "Indeed, I know that which you do not know."

Surah 2 · Verse 30 — Saheeh International

The slip and the return

Adam and his wife were settled in the Garden, given everything except one tree they were forbidden to approach. But Shaytan — the same Iblis who had refused to bow — whispered to them and caused them to slip. They ate from the tree, and were brought down from the state they had been in.

Then came the moment that defines the story of mankind. Adam did not despair, and he did not blame. He and his wife turned back to their Lord with words of repentance — words Allah Himself taught them — admitting their wrongdoing and begging His forgiveness and mercy, lest they be among the losers.

Verse

قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْخَـٰسِرِينَ ﴿٢٣﴾

They said, "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."

Surah 7 · Verse 23 — Saheeh International

The Lesson Adam (AS) shows us that sin is not the end of the story — despair is. When we err, we return to Allah with the very words He taught the first man, and find Him Forgiving and Merciful.
